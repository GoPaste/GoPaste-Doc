# Themes

GoPaste supports both dark and light themes to match your system preference or personal taste.

## Switching Themes

Open **Settings** from the system tray icon, and toggle between:

- **Dark mode** — easier on the eyes in low-light environments
- **Light mode** — clean and crisp for bright environments

The theme applies instantly without restarting the application.
